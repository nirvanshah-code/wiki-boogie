WikiBoogie

Hop from one Wikipedia article to another using only the links inside each page. Reach the target in as few clicks and as little time as you can.

  1. You get a starting article and a target article.
  2. Click links inside the article you are reading. No search box.
  3. Land on the target. Your time, clicks and route are your score.

Articles load live from Wikipedia and are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
